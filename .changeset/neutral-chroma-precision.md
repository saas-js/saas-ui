Keep chroma precision on the neutrals generated by `createAppearance`.

Colors were rounded to three decimals. Neutrals scale the base chroma down to a few thousandths, so the ramp collapsed onto two values: the canvas and panels rounded down while the foreground and border overlays rounded up, leaving the canvas the least tinted surface with over-tinted neutrals drawn on it. Chroma now keeps five decimals, which matches what the CSS contract already resolved in the browser.
